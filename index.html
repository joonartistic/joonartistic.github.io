Statement

My work begins with personal experience. The quality of light in a forest, a landscape encountered while traveling, the discomfort of a dental procedure, the textures of everyday life find their way onto clay. The hand-built form becomes the outline of an experience, and the brushwork follows the way memory surfaces. I work at the boundary of ceramics and painting, exploring the moment when vivid experience becomes material.
